Setup

Classifier & Analysis Settings

Tag model entities as sources (shadow casters) or receptors (the surfaces you care about). Configure location, date ranges, and grid resolution once — Parasolv remembers it for the rest of your study.

Lite Pro Studio
Screenshot: Shadow Fan overlay with hourly bands from sunrise to sunset

Shadow Analysis

Shadow Fan & Graphics

The core of the plugin. Sweep the sun across any day or season and see the shadow envelope live on your model. Tune gradient, opacity, and banding from the Graphics panel without leaving the workflow.

Pro Studio
Screenshot: PV assessment heatmap on a roof surface

Solar Impact

PV Assessment

Evaluate photovoltaic potential on any face using the same solar model as your shadow studies. Great for early-stage feasibility on roofs, facades, and canopies.

Pro Studio
Screenshot: Batch progress panel running scenarios overnight

Processing

Batch & Mask Mode

Batch runs a queue of configurations end-to-end. Mask Mode hides non-essential geometry during analysis to speed up heavy models. Studio users can schedule batches to run overnight.

Pro (Mask only) Studio (full Batch)

Design Tools

Design Envelope Generation

Back-solve the largest volume a site can support without overshadowing defined receptors. Export the envelope as a SketchUp group for massing studies and feasibility reports.

Scenario Management & CSV Export

Layer variants on top of a baseline. Compare receptor-level metrics at a glance, then export structured CSVs ready for planning submissions, compliance reports, or client markdown.
