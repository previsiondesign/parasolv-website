Parasolv in Practice

Prevision Design FastCast

Parasolv has been developed over a decade as the in-house software for Prevision Design and FastCast and served as the engine for shadow studies in dozens of municipalities from California to Texas to New York. After years of internal development and innovation, Parasolv is now being offered publicly for the first time.

Parasolv features

Every tool a shadow study needs, all available inside SketchUp.

⚙

Intuitive scenario based setup

Parasolv works by learning what shadow conditions you want to compare — from a city neighborhood before and after a new tower to a backyard before and after a second story addition. With simple, intuitive setup options you will be ready to go in minutes.

◈

Incredible flexibility

Which days do you want to study? Which daylight hours are important to you? Want to study the full year, or just a single season? Parasolv can accommodate whatever parameters your study needs.

◴

Time Step Graphics

Generate an image series showing the movement of shadows over time, with color coding to clearly differentiate the new shadows from existing ones.

☀

Shadow Aggregates

See the full range of locations where shadows fall across any day, season, or even a full year — all layered into one informative and visually stunning graphic, color coded to show the range of shadow durations.

⌖

Quantitative Analysis

Need to know detailed information about the effects of shadow on a specific area such as a city park or a bedroom window? Just assign it as a receptor and Parasolv can generate a full and customizable impact report.

▦

PV Assessment beta

Evaluate the effect of shadow on photovoltaic installations using historical solar output data from select systems to gain a real-world understanding of the impact.

◫

Shadow Shaper beta

Test, evaluate and refine massing volumes and see their shadow effects on any area that may be sensitive to shadow. A powerful design and diagnostic tool.

⟳

Advanced Workflow Tools

Advanced tools to expedite workflows for large projects: Batch Processing to sequence multiple outputs to run concurrently, and Mask Mode which allows multiple processes to leverage the same shadow data for greater speed and efficiency.

Shadow Fan

See the whole day in one glance.

The Shadow Fan visualization overlays shadow positions across a chosen time range onto your model. Check solar access on a public space, sun-hour compliance on a neighboring window, or seasonal exposure on a PV array — without rendering a single frame.

Explore Shadow Fan

Receptors & Results

Data you can put in a report.

Tag neighboring buildings, open space, or individual facades as receptors. Parasolv computes sun-hours, shadow-hours, and overshadowing metrics — then exports clean CSVs for planning submissions.

For when one study isn't enough.

The Studio tier adds batch processing and design-envelope generation — so you can explore dozens of massing options, or back-solve the largest building the site can support without overshadowing its neighbors.
