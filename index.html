parasolv shadow

The sun-shadow analysis toolbox. Generate fast, accurate, visually compelling shadow studies — shadow fans, hour-by-hour sweeps, net-new-shadow comparisons — without leaving SketchUp.

  • NOAA-grade solar positioning for any date, time and place
  • Purpose-built for CEQA, Section 295 and municipal shadow reviews
  • Lite, Pro and Studio tiers — one plugin, no silos

parasolv match

Match your SketchUp camera to any photo in seconds. True focal length, GPS placement and matching sun from the photo's own metadata — then mask foregrounds and export presentation-ready images.

  • EXIF-accurate field of view — not eyeballed
  • Exterior, interior and drone photos
  • Photo Finish masking, blending and layered PSD export

Why Parasolv

Accurate enough to defend

Built as in-house software for planning consultants first. The numbers come from the photo's metadata and from published solar algorithms, and they hold up in front of a hearing.

Never leave SketchUp

Matching, shadow sweeps, masking and export all happen in the model. No Photoshop round-trips, no second application to finish the job.

Made by practitioners

A decade of daily use on real projects in California, Texas and New York before being offered publicly. Parasolv is published by Berm Labs.
