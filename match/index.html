How it works

New — Photo Finish

From matched view to finished image — still inside SketchUp

A matched camera is half the job. Photo Finish composites your model INTO the photo: real foreground objects pass in front of the model, edges blend, and the result exports at the photo's full resolution.

MASK

Paint the foreground

Brush and polygon tools mask the photo's trees, fences and street furniture so they read in front of your model — with per‑edge feathering and full undo.

BLEND

Make it sit in the shot

Line‑weight, brightness, contrast, saturation, noise and blur controls tune the model render to the photograph. Crop to standard or custom aspects.

EXPORT

Deliver at full resolution

Flattened JPEG / PNG / TIFF, or a layered PSD that opens in Photoshop with photo, model and an editable foreground mask on separate layers.
