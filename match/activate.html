Install & activate

Your license key was emailed to you after checkout (if you don't see it, check your junk/spam folder). Here's how to get up and running.

1. Install the extension

If you haven't already, install Parasolv Match in SketchUp:

  • From the Extension Warehouse (search "Parasolv Match"), or
  • Download the .rbz and use Extensions → Extension Manager → Install Extension…

2. Activate your key

  1. In SketchUp, open the Camera menu → Parasolv Match → License.
  2. Click Enter / Activate key…, paste the license key from your email, and click OK.
  3. You'll see "Parasolv Match activated. Thank you!" — that's it.
Installed before the rename? Older builds list the same menu as ViewMatch Pro rather than Parasolv Match. Everything else is identical.
Your license covers 2 machines. Activating uses one slot per computer. The plugin validates online occasionally and keeps working through short offline periods.

Troubleshooting

"Activation failed" — double‑check the key (copy the whole thing), confirm you're online, and make sure you haven't already activated two machines. Still stuck? Email support@parasolv.com with your order email and the message you saw.

Managing your subscription — use the link in your purchase receipt to update payment or cancel. After a cancellation your license keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

One-month licenses — the non-renewing option simply expires on its end date. Nothing is charged again, and nothing happens unless you buy another term.
