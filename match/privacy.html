Parasolv Match is a SketchUp extension published by Berm Labs LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the website and the extension handle.

Website

This site is static and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our host (Cloudflare) may process standard server logs (e.g., IP address, request metadata) to deliver and secure the site.

The extension

  • Your photos stay local. EXIF metadata is read on your computer to match the camera; photos are not uploaded to us.
  • License validation. To activate and validate a license, the extension sends your license key and a non-reversible machine fingerprint (a hash of basic device identifiers) to our licensing provider, Keygen (keygen.sh).
  • Update checks. The extension may contact the internet to check for updates to its bundled EXIF reader.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Polar, our Merchant of Record, which collects the billing and contact information needed to complete your purchase, deliver your license key, and remit taxes. See Polar's and Keygen's privacy policies for how they handle that data.

Email

We use your email address to deliver your license key and provide support. We don't sell your information.

Your choices & contact

To access or delete your data, or for any privacy question, contact support@parasolv.com.
