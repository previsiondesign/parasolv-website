Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Parasolv Match is a SketchUp extension published by Berm Labs LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the website and the extension handle.

Website

This site (parasolv.com/match) is static and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our host (Cloudflare) may process standard server logs (e.g., IP address, request metadata) to deliver and secure the site.

The extension

  • Your photos stay local. EXIF metadata is read on your computer to match the camera; photos are not uploaded to us.
  • License validation. To activate and validate a license, the extension sends your license key and a machine fingerprint to our licensing provider, Keygen (keygen.sh). The fingerprint is a one-way hash of your operating system platform, your computer’s name and your operating-system account name — we receive the hash, not those values. When you first activate a computer, its name and platform are also sent in readable form, so that an activation can be identified in your account. Nothing else about your device is collected.
  • Update checks. The extension periodically checks exiftool.org for a newer version of its bundled EXIF reader and installs it when one is available, so a file is downloaded from that third-party site and not only a version number; when you choose Check for Updates it also asks our update server (api.parasolv.com) for the latest plugin version. We send no personal data in either case, but those servers see standard request metadata such as your IP address.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Polar, our Merchant of Record, which collects the billing and contact information needed to complete your purchase, deliver your license key, and remit taxes. See Polar's and Keygen's privacy policies for how they handle that data.

Email

We use your email address to deliver your license key, to send end-of-term reminders if you bought a fixed-term license, and to provide support. Those messages are delivered through Resend (resend.com), which processes your address on our behalf. We don't sell your information.

Your choices & contact

To access or delete your data, or for any privacy question, contact support@parasolv.com.
